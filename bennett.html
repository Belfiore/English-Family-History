Lilian Bennett's family

Lilian Maud Mary Bennett, Pat's mother, was born about March 1900 at Bodmin. She was the youngest surviving child of Charles Bennett and Annie, probably Matilda Ann Nicholls Grigg.

Lilian Maud Mary FitzMaurice, nee Bennett

About March 1900, Bodmin. Probably died late 1947, Uxbridge district, aged 47.

The birth index, June quarter 1900, Bodmin, volume 5c, page 64, names her mother as Grigg. In June 1921 she was 21 years and 3 months, born at Bodmin, a clerk at the Claims Record Office, boarding at Hampton Hill. She probably married Baron Reginald FitzMaurice in the December quarter of 1927, Hammersmith, volume 1a, page 661. She probably died in the December quarter of 1947, Uxbridge, volume 5f, page 103, aged 47. The Uxbridge district covered Hayes, which is why the 1950 album calls her the late Mrs R. Fitzmaurice. No will and no grave have been found. A memory that she died while Pat was a young child does not fit an age of about 18.

Charles and Annie

Charles Bennett was born at Egloshayle, Cornwall, about 1849 to 1851. The 1911 census says Slades, Egloshayle. He was a tailor in 1891, a general labourer in 1901, and a tailor and outfitter in 1911. Annie was probably Matilda Ann Nicholls Grigg, born in the St Columb district in the March quarter of 1864, volume 5c, page 131, mother's maiden name Stephens. They probably married in the September quarter of 1882, in the Stoke Damerel district, volume 5b, page 573, where Charles Bennett and Annie Grigg share a page.

In 1911 they were living apart in Bodmin. Annie, who kept a draper's shop, described herself as a widow, though Charles was alive in the same town with their son Frank. Both forms say 11 children had been born, 8 were living and 3 had died. Annie died in the June quarter of 1912 at Bodmin, aged 48, registered as Matilda Ann Nicholls Bennett, volume 5c, page 89. Charles probably died at Bodmin on 5 December 1925, aged 77. Administration of an estate of 296 pounds 1 shilling and 4 pence was granted to May Bennett, a spinster. That grant does not show which daughter she was.

The eleven children

All eleven births were registered at Bodmin with mother's maiden name Grigg. Three died as infants, which matches the 1911 count.

  • James Alfred, late 1883, volume 5c, page 79. Probably died early 1884, aged 0.
  • Gilbert Nicholls, late 1884, volume 5c, page 77. A tailor's apprentice in 1901, and a bootfitter in 1911.
  • Charles Reginald, late 1886, volume 5c, page 71. A draper's apprentice in 1901. The census calls him Reginald.
  • Minnie Elizabeth, spring 1888, volume 5c, page 72. A milliner, single, with her mother in 1911. She is not Amanda's Auntie May.
  • Harry, spring 1890, volume 5c, page 77.
  • Arthur Sidney, spring 1892, volume 5c, page 65. Called Sidney in 1901. He is not Sidney George Andrews.
  • Frederick Cyril, spring 1894, volume 5c, page 77. Probably died in 1895, aged 1.
  • William Frances, late 1895, volume 5c, page 69. Known as Francis, and as Frank. With his father in 1911.
  • Cecil Floyd Chester, summer 1898, volume 5c, page 70. Called Floyd. He probably married Ethel G. Clark in 1924, in the Farnham district, and he probably died in 1970, in the Plymouth district.
  • Lilian Maud Mary, about March 1900, volume 5c, page 64.
  • Harold Victor, spring 1902, volume 5c, page 61. Probably died in 1904, aged 1.
