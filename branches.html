Patricia Andrews, nee FitzMaurice, and Ronald Andrews have both died. Ronald died in 2010 (family). Pat died in 2020 (family). Pat and Ron are buried together in the churchyard of St John the Baptist Parish Church, Waterloo Road, Crowthorne, Berkshire (family). Patricia was probably born in the Hammersmith district in early 1929, and the family remembers Windsor, which is not the registered birthplace. Their years are on the tree. Everyone else who may still be living is named with their relationship only. House numbers are left off. The 1890 London Gazette rules out a descent from that family. A Carlow origin for John FitzMaurice, which would make the earls distant cousins rather than ancestors, is likely but not proved.
