Research notebook

Research notes and leads

This page keeps everything the research has found and saved, so nothing is lost: each find with its label, the reference, a link to the original and a picture of the entry with the exact line highlighted. Much of it is not proved. Leads, low confidence guesses and things ruled out are kept here and are not stated as facts on the family pages. The finding list for the family pages is on the Sources page.

How the labels work

  • FACT A record states it, read from the original or from two independent sources.
  • PROBABLE The records point the same way, but a certificate or a second reading would still help.
  • LEAD Worth following up. A clue, a family story, a published tree or an index entry not yet tied to the family.
  • LOW CONFIDENCE A possible match with real doubts.
  • CONFLICT Two sources disagree. Both readings are kept until a record settles it.
  • RULED OUT Checked and shown not to belong to this family, or not to be the person.
  • UNKNOWN Searched for and not found, or not yet answerable.

Each item carries the number it has in the research ledger (C0001 onwards). People who may still be living are named with their relationship only. Their records stay in the private research files.
