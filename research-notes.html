Research notebook

Research notes and leads

This page keeps everything the research has found and saved, so nothing is lost: each find with its label, the reference, a link to the original and a picture of the entry with the exact line highlighted. Much of it is not proved. Leads, low confidence guesses and things ruled out are kept here and are not stated as facts on the family pages. The finding list for the family pages is on the Sources page.

How the labels work

  • FACT A record states it, read from the original or from two independent sources.
  • PROBABLE The records point the same way, but a certificate or a second reading would still help.
  • LEAD Worth following up. A clue, a family story, a published tree or an index entry not yet tied to the family.
  • LOW CONFIDENCE A possible match with real doubts.
  • CONFLICT Two sources disagree. Both readings are kept until a record settles it.
  • RULED OUT Checked and shown not to belong to this family, or not to be the person.
  • UNKNOWN Searched for and not found, or not yet answerable.

Each item carries the number it has in the research ledger (C0001 onwards). People who may still be living are named with their relationship only. Their records stay in the private research files.

New on 26 November 2026: John FitzMaurice and his family

Recent notes, C0689 to C0701

  • C0689 PROBABLE Four of the Bennett and Ellison children were registered again, late, in 1933 and 1934, with cross references in the original quarters. The births and names of children who may be living are kept to names only.
  • C0690 PROBABLE As C0689, at Epsom: two older children were re-registered in the September quarter 1933.
  • C0691 LEAD Hypothesis: the Epsom and Sussex Bennett children whose mother's maiden name was Ellison are one family, the children of one couple, possibly Gilbert Nicholls Bennett, a racehorse trainer at Alfriston before 1931. Not proved. Possibly living, named only: Patricia, Jean F, Anne, Ivor N A, Mary and Peter. Gilbert (1929 to 1976) has died.
  • C0692 PROBABLE FreeBMD lists every Gilbert Bennett birth 1926 to 1932: Gilbert E (mother Ireland), Redruth Q1 1926; Gilbert F (Hylands), Hailsham Q1 1926; Gilbert (Tanner), Newtown Q1 1927; Gilbert (Ellison), Brighton Q2 1929; Gilbert K (Collins), Newton Abbot Q4 1932. There is no Gilbert Bennett born in 1930 anywhere in England and Wales. A child born in February 1929 would normally be registered in the June quarter 1929. The 1976 death entry reads "-FE1930" (month and year only). With the unique full name Gilbert Nicholls, the Brighton registry for his administration (C0666) and no other candidate, the Gravesend man is probably the Brighton Gilbert of 1929 and the 1930 year in the death index is a one year error (informant or index). Not proven: only a certificate or the administration grant would settle it
  • C0693 LEAD FreeBMD has no marriage for any Gilbert N or Gilbert Nicholls Bennett, 1837 to 1992. Plain "Gilbert" Bennett marriages 1945 to 1976 are all outside Kent and Sussex (Kingsbridge 1946, Welshpool 1946, Nelson 1961, Beverley 1976). So the younger Gilbert (died 1976, administration, estate £960) was probably unmarried, which fits an administration rather than a will; his administrator would then be a parent or sibling. The elder Gilbert's marriage is also not found (consistent with C0691's late registrations); the only Gilbert Nicholls birth in the index is Q4 1884 Bodmin 5c 77
  • C0694 LEAD A marriage index entry for John C V Bennett, Cecil's probable son, was found. He may be living, so the details stay in the private research files.
  • C0695 UNKNOWN No free online electoral registers were found for the three households.   • C0696 LEAD The compiled site says Robert Thomas FitzMaurice, born 2 June 1808 (son of John of Kelvin Grove and Henrietta Frances Cary), married Elizabeth Georgiana Evans at St Mary, Lambeth, on 17 October 1833 "when he was in Ordnance Office, Dublin", died 31 May 1837 at Surrey Lodge, Rathmines, Dublin; his widow "married again to Frederick Branston of Clapham"; daughters Georgiana Emily (1835, died young) and Emma Frances (born 27 March 1837, Surrey Lodge, Rathmines). A separate WikiTree profile independently quotes an 1833 marriage notice: Georgiana Elizabeth Evans "m. 1833, Robert Thomas FitzMaurice, Esq. of the Ordnance-office, Castle, Dublin", and gives her as daughter of Percival Evans of Tooting and Clapham, 1805 to 1866
  • C0697 PROBABLE FreeBMD: Georgiana E Branston died Q3 1866, Wandsworth 1d 302, aged 61 (born about 1805). This fits Georgiana Elizabeth Evans (born 1805), Robert Thomas FitzMaurice's widow, remarried to Frederick Branston of Clapham (C0696); Clapham is in Wandsworth registration district, and Emma Frances was "of St John's Clapham" at her 1859 marriage (C0507). No Frederick Branston or Branson marriage 1837 to 1858 is in FreeBMD, so the remarriage was probably in Ireland or under another spelling
  • C0698 PROBABLE FreeBMD: Emma Fitzmaurice Dixon was born Q3 1860, Lambeth 1d 375 (daughter of Samuel Dixon and Emma Frances, C0507) and married George Minchin Luther, Q2 1890, Wandsworth 1d 963 (both names on the same page). The compiled site makes George Minchin Luther (born 1846, Crumlin) a son of Guy Luther of Clonmel and Alicia Maria FitzMaurice, a daughter of John FitzMaurice of Kelvin Grove, Carlow, so Emma was marrying her mother's first cousin's family. If the Luther parentage is right, this is a documented-in-the-index tie between Robert Thomas's London descendants and the Carlow family, independent of the compiler's say-so
  • C0699 LEAD Best reading: Emma Frances (born 1837) was John's niece, the daughter of his younger brother Robert Thomas, and so a first cousin of Robert William (1832). For: both men were clerks of the Board of Ordnance in the same years (John at the Tower, 1827 to 1832, FACT, C0515; Robert Thomas in the Ordnance Office, Dublin, 1833, secondary, C0696); Robert Thomas married in Lambeth and his widow and daughter lived at Clapham, the same Wandsworth district where Robert William married in 1861 (Battersea) and Emma Frances married in 1859; Emma Frances's daughter married into the Carlow family (C0698); Robert William named a son Edward Dixon (1872). Against: no record yet names John and Robert Thomas as brothers, and no FitzMaurice signed at the 1859 wedding
  • C0700 UNKNOWN Still not found in any primary record: John's birthplace, age or parents. Searched: FreeBMD deaths John Fitzmaurice 1837 to 1912 (two unlinked entries not in C0517: St Martin in the Fields Q3 1844, 1 105, and Brentford Q4 1865, 3a 51 and 61, probably the Chiswick John of 1841); TNA Discovery (no item-level index naming Fitzmaurice among Ordnance clerks; WO 54 establishment books are not searchable by name online); Internet Archive full text (no Ordnance Office list naming him). Not done: Irish church records on irishgenealogy.ie (Cloudflare check; not bypassed); FreeBMD scan pages (browser check; not bypassed); Ancestry (no signed-in browser available in this session)
  • C0701 LEAD (1) Descent from any Earl of Orkney: RULED OUT (C0505 stands; the Orkney title and Hamilton blood came through Mary O'Brien, and no John born 1790 to 1810 exists in the earls' male lines). (2) Male-line FitzMaurice (Kerry) cousinship with Cecil O'Bryen FitzMaurice, 8th Earl (1919 to 1998): LEAD, Moderate (up from Low). The London generation is fully documented; the Robert Thomas branch, his widow's death in Wandsworth (C0697) and the Luther marriage (C0698) make the compiler's Carlow placement of John more likely; but no record ties John the Tower clerk to John born 6 July 1803 in Carlow.
